How BenefitShield Works

Use the tools below to prepare before applying for a government benefit.

01

Find a Scheme

Search the available schemes and read their basic eligibility, benefits and document requirements.

02

Complete Your Profile

Add your name, mobile number, Aadhaar number, community, caste, religion and school type.

03

Check Documents

Upload a document and use the basic document checker to review readability and profile-name matching.

04

Check Suspicious Messages

Paste a message into Fraud Check to identify common warning signs such as OTP, PIN and payment requests.

Important Safety Reminder

BenefitShield provides assistance and basic checks. Always verify the final eligibility requirements, application process and official contact information through the relevant government website.

Never share your OTP, PIN, password or other confidential authentication information.
